# The Ship

## Game

Between Runs I stand on my own Ship, in first person, hands empty until I buy something. This is the only place Salvage gets spent and the only way back to the Bastion.

Three hatch doors along one side. One opens per seat. The party lead owns the EMBARK, and each seat shops their own loadout.

Four consoles and each owns exactly one subject, so I always know where a thing is bought. Weapons and their wheels at the ARMORY. The protocol I wear at CREW QUARTERS. The Hatch, the Repair Rig, the equipment slot and the Decoy at FABRICATION. Where I go, and the button that commits me, at the NAV MAP.

Buy the free Secondary first. It costs nothing and it is sold rather than given so the first thing I learn is which console weapons come from. Then claim a protocol, which also costs nothing at its plainest.

Weapon Tiers and the equipment slot stay shut until Salvage first converts through the Hatch. Nothing else waits.

The NAV MAP will warn me if I own no weapon and no protocol, and then let me go anyway. It is willing to let me make a bad bet. That is consistent with everything else here.

## Reference

Between runs you are stood in your own ship, in first person. Hands are empty until you buy a weapon at the ARMORY. You cannot fire here either way. This is the only place Salvage gets spent, and the only way back to the Bastion.

You walk. You look at a console until the prompt appears at the bottom of the screen, you hold interact, and a panel opens: your movement locks, the cursor comes free, and the panel owns the screen until you close it. One panel at a time. Close it and you are walking again. Binding: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

Every panel, whichever console it belongs to, shows your banked Salvage at the top. A row you cannot afford does not grey out — it tells you exactly how much more you need. That is deliberate: buying something you cannot pay for is not a silent refusal.

The header in the corner of the screen lists which direction each console is in. If you are lost, read it.

### What each console is for

Each console owns exactly one subject. Nothing is sold in two places.

**ARMORY — the weapon in your hands.** Weapons, and the upgrade wheel for a weapon you already own. This is also the only place you decide which owned weapon fills your Primary slot and which fills your Secondary; in the Bastion the weapon keys only swap between the slots you are already carrying, never between things you own. Buying a weapon equips it into its own slot straight away. Upgrades are bought per weapon, so Salvage poured into one gun stays in that gun — buy a different Primary and its upgrade wheel starts at nothing.

**CREW QUARTERS — you.** The protocol you embark wearing: doctrine, Crew ability on
`G`, Personal on `H`, and a passive where the sheet has one. Claim Default at cost 0,
or claim **VEIL** or **FORGE**. AEGIS and LEDGER are not sold. Repairing the Hatch
is not here; that is the Repair Rig at FABRICATION.

**FABRICATION — the Hatch.** How much the door can take before it falls, plus the equipment slot, the Repair Rig, and the Decoy that fills the Drone bay. What it buys applies from your next embark onward, not retroactively.

**NAV MAP — where you go, and going there.** One destination. A selector for which Wave you start on, capped by the deepest Wave you have cleared. And the EMBARK button, which is the point of no return: once the Bastion loads, the only exits are clearing it and losing. On a friends session only the party lead's EMBARK commits the party.

### The start-Wave selector

The NAV MAP will let you begin partway in. It does not skip anything — starting deeper delivers every Wave up to that point back to back, with no Prep in between, and then the Run continues normally to the end. You get the same Run with the early half compressed into one shapeless fight. It is a way to stop replaying Wave one, not a shortcut, and the overlap is the price.

### Embarking with nothing

The NAV MAP will warn you loudly if you own no weapon or no protocol and then let you go anyway. You are not blocked. No weapon means the improvised cutter. No protocol means `G` and `H` refuse. The game is willing to let you make a bad bet; that is consistent with everything else in it.

### What to buy first

**The free sidearm at the ARMORY, before anything.** It costs nothing, and the only reason it is sold rather than given is so that the first thing you learn is that weapons come from that console.

**Then CREW QUARTERS.** Claim Default — it costs nothing, same reason the sidearm is
sold rather than given. Weapon upgrades and the equipment slot are locked until Salvage
first converts through the Hatch. Hatch Armor at FABRICATION is also open from the first minute.

**Then whatever killed you.** If the Hatch fell while you were still standing, that is FABRICATION and not you at all.

**Save the big weapon purchase for when you know which fight you want.** A Primary is fixed for the whole Run, and its upgrade wheel does not follow you to the next gun. Buying one is committing to a range and a cadence, not just spending money.
