# Upgrades

## Game

Permanent, campaign-wide. CREW QUARTERS sells the **Boarder Protocol** you wear —
Default, VEIL, or FORGE. FABRICATION changes the Hatch — how much it can absorb, the
Repair Rig without which you cannot repair at all, and the Decoy that fills the Drone bay.

Weapon Tiers and the equipment slot stay locked until Salvage first converts through the Hatch. Claiming Default and Hatch Armor do not. Costs and curves: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

## Reference

Permanent, campaign-wide, and bought at two consoles. Nothing here is lost when a Run ends badly. Once a level is bought it is yours for good.

The one remaining **track** works the same way it always did: each level costs more than the last, and each level buys the same size of step. That track is Hatch Armor. Everything else at these consoles is a one-off, a payload, or a protocol pick.

### CREW QUARTERS — the protocol you embark wearing

There are no attribute tracks. Vitality, Stamina, Scavenging and Luck were refunded and deleted. Health, sprint, Salvage per kill and crit chance do not move from this console.

You claim a **Boarder Protocol** at this console. Default costs 0. **VEIL** and
**FORGE** are also claimable. Bindings for the bursts: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

**Default — STOCK.** Decoys place as sold. **Barrage** (`G`): plant, rooted, your gun
and nearby Boarders dump fire. **Rush** (`H`): your gun only, you still move. No Passive.

**VEIL — PHANTOM.** A decoy you place holds the aggro you are carrying. **Ping** (`G`):
the room marks Gatekeepers for the crew and pulls Defaults and Hunters onto you.
**Cloak** (`H`): off targeting lists until you fire. Passive **Ghost**: they notice you
closer in.

**FORGE — PARALLEL BAYS.** Two decoys may stand live. **Field Deploy** (`G`): stand a
turret where you look. **Overcharge** (`H`): redline that turret (and yourself) for a
window. Passive **Magnet Hands**: hunters that would take you take the turret instead.

AEGIS and LEDGER are not on the panel.

### FABRICATION — the tracks that change the door

**Hatch Armor** raises how much the Hatch can absorb before the Run ends. It applies from your next embark, so buying it changes nothing you can see standing on the Ship.

**The Repair Rig is not a track.** It is a one-off piece of equipment at FABRICATION: buy the equipment slot, buy the Rig, and equip it. It repairs at a flat rate and nothing raises that rate. Without it equipped you cannot repair the Hatch at all. Both prices and the rate are in the [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

**The Decoy** fills the Drone bay. It is a payload, not a gun, and it is sold here — not at the ARMORY. Ownership and charges: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

### What is gated behind a Hatch conversion, and what is not

**Weapon Tiers at the ARMORY and the equipment slot at FABRICATION are both locked until Salvage first converts through the Hatch** — a Hatch-bank drip or dump that credits the bank. Docked or Lost after that does not matter; the conversion is the key. Everything else is buyable from the first minute.

The protocol at CREW QUARTERS, Hatch Armor at FABRICATION, the Decoy, and buying the weapons themselves are all available from the first minute of a fresh campaign. Gear that waits until you have banked once is the wheel and the Rig's slot — until then you do not yet know what you need.

### What is actually worth buying

**Hatch Armor when you are losing Runs with your health bar full.** That is a clean signal: the Run ended at the door, not at you, and no amount of personal upgrade fixes it.

**The Repair Rig only if you intend to repair at all** — and be honest that repairing is treating the symptom. The Rig does not make repair *cheaper*; it is what makes repair possible, and the Salvage bill per point healed is unchanged by owning it. If you are repairing a lot, the real fix is killing Thieves further from the door.

**The Decoy if you need something to pull them off the Hatch** during a Hatch-bank. It is one payload per Run.

**Ghost if they keep spotting you down a corridor.** Foresight if you want the mix named before you juice.

**Weapon upgrades are the deepest hole in the game and the money does not come back out.** An upgrade wheel belongs to one weapon. Take a gun a long way and then decide you wanted the other one, and you start from nothing. Only commit once you know which Primary you actually want to be carrying.
