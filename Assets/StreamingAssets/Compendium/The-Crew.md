# The Crew

## Game

The room counts us.

A realm is opened from the chooser: OPEN TO FRIENDS, and a join code to read out. Friends land on the Ship — this world's Ship — and shop their own loadout out of the world's one pot. The party lead owns EMBARK. Everyone else readies.

Arrive after the party has gone and nothing is wasted. The Ship holds me, the prompt names the next break, and FOLLOW puts me aboard at that break, free. My own hatch door opens as I arrive.

The garrison answers the number of us standing, and it answers at the break, never mid-Wave. A Wave already on the floor stays the Wave it was, whoever arrives or leaves. The next one is sized for whoever is actually in the room.

More eyes is the real change. Two of us can hold two Cloakers. A crew burst has a crew to land on. And every Default that flips is flipping onto somebody — the room's attention becomes a thing we pass between us.

Going down puts a seat back on the Ship, waiting, with a priced way back in. The price comes out of the world's pot, so a crew that does not die is a crew that can still afford to.

## Reference

**Opening.** OPEN TO FRIENDS on the chooser (or HOST ON LAN as the fallback) hands out a join code once the realm allows it — a brand-new realm refuses until its first wheel purchase, or the opening stretch ending. JOIN on the main menu takes the code. The party shares the Ship; each seat shops itself; only the party lead's EMBARK commits the party. Joins during the embark countdown are refused.

**Following.** A seat that joins mid-Run lands on the Ship with a prompt naming the next break. FOLLOW during the party's Prep boards them with the party, and their hatch door opens at that moment. The same control during an Assault refuses with a reason rather than queueing silently. FOLLOW is free — that seat never died.

**Scaling.** Wave composition counts the seats playing at the moment the next Wave is composed. No Wave already on the floor changes size, health or composition when a seat joins or leaves. A leaving seat is dropped from the count at the next Wave, and its door seals at the same boundary.

**Dying.** A downed guest waits on the Ship; the wait surface and the NAV MAP price the way back in. The rule, its charge pool and its climbing price: [Going Down](Going-Down.md).
