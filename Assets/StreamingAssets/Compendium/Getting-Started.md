# First Time Out

## Game

A Wave starts at the Hatch and ends at the far terminal. That walk is the fight.

I buy the MK-1, take a protocol if I have none, and leave from the NAV MAP. I start at the Hatch, shut off at the terminal, dock or go deeper. Master Control is last: the oversized one only arms the switch. Throwing it strips the Bastion.

Health comes back between hits. The Hatch does not. FABRICATION waits until this one is stripped.

## Reference

The prompt under the crosshair names the verb. Keys: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

### On the Ship

1. **REALMS.** NEW REALM, NEW BOARDER, ENTER.
2. Hold `E` to unseal, then hold `E` on the NAV MAP.
3. Ship terminal: `E`, then `T`.
4. **ARMORY.** Buy the **MK-1**. The other free Secondary does not ready embark.
5. **CREW QUARTERS** if the panel opens. **NAV MAP → EMBARK.**

### In the Bastion

6. `T` at the **Hatch**. Walk the corridor. `T` at the **terminal**. Kills are already banked.
7. Dock on `E`, or `T` at the Hatch for the next stretch. Two Waves, then three. Same walk.
8. Field done: `T` at the Hatch is **Master Control**. Kill the oversized Gatekeeper — that only arms the switch. `T` at the Master terminal. **BASTION STRIPPED**.

Ammo does not run out. Health climbs when nothing is hitting you. Zero Hatch ends the Boarding.
