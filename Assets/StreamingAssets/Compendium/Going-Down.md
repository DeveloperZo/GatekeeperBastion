# Going Down

## Game

Going down is a bill, and the realm pays it or the Run ends.

The carry is gone either way. Unbanked dies where I fell, at any price. The pot pays to stand me up; nothing pays to hand back what I was holding.

A respawn is billed to the realm's banked Salvage, and the price climbs with every one bought this Run — whoever it was bought for. The counter is the Run's, not mine. One pot, one tab, everybody drinking.

Mid-Assault there is a ration on top: a shared stock of charges, seeded by how many of us embarked. A mid-Wave stand-up burns a charge and pays the price. Charges out, the fallen wait for the break. At the break there is no ration — only the price, still climbing.

Down, I wait behind the vignette with the offer written on it, priced. A guest waits on the Ship with the same offer. If the pot cannot pay and nobody is left standing, there is no offer. That is the whole difference between a setback and a loss.

A fresh realm has banked nothing, so its first death simply ends the Run. Credit is not extended here.

The Hatch is the exception to everything above. When the door falls there is nothing to buy. The Run is over, whatever is in the pot.

## Reference

Every dead seat — guest, host, solo — comes back through one rule: a respawn priced from the realm's banked pot, at a price that climbs per respawn taken this Run. The counter belongs to the Run and resets with it.

### The two windows

**Mid-Assault**, a respawn also burns a charge from a shared pool. The pool seeds as the Run tethers, sized by the seats that embarked, and grows when a seat follows in mid-Run. A leaving seat refunds nothing. The pool is not the price — a mid-Wave respawn burns a charge **and** pays the cost.

**At a break** (any Prep), no charge is needed and there is no limit. The price still applies and still climbs.

### The surfaces

The Boarder who owns the screen goes down into a DOWN state: a vignette with the offer named on it — the cost, or the reason it refuses. Confirm respawns them at the spawn, healed. A guest waits on the Ship; the wait surface and the NAV MAP carry FOLLOW for a seat that never embarked (free — that seat never died) and a priced RESPAWN for one that did, with named refusals when the charges are out or the pot cannot pay.

### What a respawn never does

It never refunds Unbanked — death wipes the dying seat's Unbanked, respawned or not. It never revives the Hatch: the Hatch at zero ends the Run whatever is banked. And it never changes the Wave: composition and the wallet split are untouched by a death or a return.

### When there is no offer

If no respawn can be honored — no charge mid-Wave, or a pot below the price — and no other seat is still fighting, the Run ends Lost. A fresh realm banks nothing before its first convert, so its first death is final by arithmetic, not by a special rule.
