# The Other Currencies — Coming Soon

## Game

Coming Soon.

Salvage will stop working alone. Two more currencies are on the way, and the split is clean: Salvage owns my kit, one of the others owns the world, and the last owns me.

The world's currency is the strange one. It buys the opposition — which frames garrison a Bastion, how hard they run, what they carry. I will pay to make the room worse, because a worse room pays better. Spent, never risked, never a rank.

My own currency comes out of the survived Convert — the one moment this place currently pays in nothing but relief — scaled by how deep I carried. Spent on the protocol I wear, or on the Boarding itself.

Neither has earned its name yet. Until they land, everything the [Salvage](Salvage.md) page says stays true: one currency, one pile, everything competing for it.

## Reference

The three-currency split is decided (Experience the world, Salvage the kit, Cores the self); Experience is a spendable wallet and never a rank; Cores buy protocol unlock and upgrade plus Boarding multipliers; the roster-as-purchase is scoped per Bastion, set at first boarding and cleared when Master Control falls. Nothing is built and no numbers exist. Names are working names.
