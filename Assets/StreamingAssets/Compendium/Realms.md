# Realms

## Game

A realm is the world. I am not.

The chooser comes first: REALMS, from the main menu. Realms on one list, Boarders on another. NEW REALM starts a world. NEW BOARDER starts a self. ENTER commits the pair, onto that world's Ship. The same Boarder can walk different worlds, one after another, and nothing gets confused by it.

What stays when I leave is the world's: the banked pot, Hatch Armor, the doors already opened, the deepest Wave already cleared. What travels is mine: weapons, wheel, protocol, equipment. I arrive in a fresh realm well-armed and broke.

The money is the world's and the gear is mine — wherever I bought it. Bank in a friend's realm and it lands in their pot. Buy in a friend's realm and it comes out of that same pot, onto my Boarder, and leaves with me. Both directions are one rule read twice.

A realm opened to friends is played together in that world. Everything the crew converts, whoever converted it, lands in the one pot, and repairs and respawns are paid back out of it.

Deleting a realm deletes a world. The Boarders that walked it lose nothing of themselves.

## Reference

A realm is the persistent world half of a save; a Boarder is the traveling half. The chooser (REALMS) lists both and ENTER opens the selected realm with the selected Boarder, onto that realm's Ship. The return from a Run lands there too.

**Realm-side**: banked Salvage and the lifetime ledger, Hatch Armor, hatch conversions, completed terminals and open doors, the deepest Wave cleared, the run counters, and the first-time gates. **Boarder-side**: weapons owned, each weapon's wheel, the protocol claimed, equipment and payloads.

**The pot is the world's.** Every seat in a session banks into the realm being played. Spending is the mirror: purchases debit that realm's pot even when the thing bought is Boarder-side and leaves with the buyer. Respawns are debited from the same pot ([Going Down](Going-Down.md)).

A fresh realm starts at the beginning: nothing banked, the first-time gates closed, and friends refused until the first wheel purchase on that realm (or the opening stretch ending). OPEN TO FRIENDS on the chooser hands out a join code once the realm allows it ([The Crew](The-Crew.md)).
