# Intensity

## Game

Intensity is a wager, placed per Wave, before I commit.

Raising it multiplies what each body pays and how many bodies arrive. Both halves move together and they read off the same table. They cannot come apart. This is the cleanest mechanism on the Bastion and I do not fully understand why it was built this honest.

It does not toughen anything. It does not tighten the spacing. Every extra body is a Default. Never a Hunter, never a Thief. A raised Wave is the same fight with more of the easiest thing in it.

Set it in Prep, at the trigger, before the select. Once Assault begins it is locked. Turning it down is free and refunds nothing.

Three questions before I raise it. Can I kill the surplus before it reaches me. What will my health look like afterwards, given it does not come back. And is the Hatch already hurt, because repair comes out of the same bank the extra pay lands in. Intensity pays gross. The Hatch charges net.

Start deep and one juice governs the whole chain. I learned that the expensive way.

## Reference

Look at a field terminal, Master Control, or the Hatch. Juice raises intensity — each step spends Unbanked. How much: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md). Select the value, including intensity 0, then start the Wave. Select again unlocks juice. Without a select the Wave does not start. Bindings: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

Lowering is free. Nothing comes back. Locked during Assault. One juice at Master Control covers the whole sequence.

### What it changes

Raising intensity multiplies what each Gatekeeper pays, and how many the next Wave sends. Both sides read the same table. Payout and body count cannot come apart.

### What it does not change

- Gatekeepers are not tougher. Health is set by which Wave you are on.
- They do not arrive faster or in bigger groups. Group size and the gap between groups belong to the Wave.
- Every extra body is a Default. Intensity cannot add a Hunter or Thief.

A raised intensity is the same fight with more of the easiest thing in it, and more money per body.

### When you can set it

Only in Prep, looking at the trigger, before select. Juice refuses after that until select again. Starting a Wave locks it for the whole Assault. That is deliberate: juice is a pre-commitment, not something you back out of when the room gets loud.

If you start deep from the NAV MAP, the chain is one Assault — juice before that first start governs every Wave in it.

The prompt prices the next step before you confirm: what a kill pays, how many bodies. Read it. The curve is in the [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

### How to think about raising it

Ask three questions, in this order.

**1. Can you kill the surplus before it reaches you?** Extra bodies hunt you on a field-terminal Wave. Your ceiling is how much you can put out between a group spawning and that group closing. If you are already finishing Waves hurt, more bodies means more chip.

**2. What is your health going to look like afterwards?** Health does not come back during a Run. A bigger Wave is more swings, and that damage carries into every Wave after it. Intensity's cost is paid across the rest of the Run, not just this Wave.

**3. Is the Hatch already hurt?** Repairing costs banked Salvage. If you raise intensity and the door takes more damage, some of the extra income goes straight back out through the repair. Intensity pays gross; the door charges net.

Juice spends Unbanked you have not Hatch-banked yet.

Some practical shapes:

- **Early Waves are the cheap place to raise it.** They send the fewest bodies and the softest ones.
- **Late Waves are where a raise actually hurts**, because the surplus lands on top of a Wave that already has the most Hunters and Thieves in it and the toughest bodies.
- **Intensity you can't finish is worth nothing.** Losing the Hatch mid-Wave stops the income immediately; extra pay only lands on Gatekeepers you actually kill.
- **Turning it back down is free.** No refund. Nothing commits you to a setting across Waves.

### The one thing intensity is not

It is not a difficulty setting for the Run. It is a per-Wave bet, and the game does not remember it. Every Prep at every trigger is a fresh decision.
