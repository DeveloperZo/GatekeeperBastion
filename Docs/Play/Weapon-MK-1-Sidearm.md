# MK-1 Sidearm

Your **ranged Secondary**. Free at the ARMORY, and still bought there. The other free Secondary is **SEC-D** (contact).

Numbers: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

## What it is for

Two jobs, and they are different jobs.

**Early, it is your whole loadout** if you picked reach over contact. Before you can afford a Primary you fight the Bastion with this. It fires a single accurate shot at a workable cadence, out to a distance that covers most of a corridor.

**Later, it is the bridge.** Because every slot carries its own clip and its own reload, the sidearm is what you fire while your Primary is empty. Swapping to the sidearm and continuing to shoot is nearly always better than standing still through a long reload with something walking at you — and with the MK-1 that bridge still works at range.

## How it wants to be used

- **Swap to it instead of reloading under pressure.** Dry Primary plus a Gatekeeper inside your face means swap, not reload.
- **Then reload the Primary at your own moment.** But remember: switching back to the Primary while the *sidearm* is mid-reload throws that progress away, and the reverse is also true. Do one thing at a time.
- **Aim it.** It fires one round per pull with no scatter, so a miss is a whole round of nothing. It rewards standing still and placing shots more than either Primary does.
- **Use it on things that are already nearly dead.** Finishing a wounded Gatekeeper is exactly its damage bracket, and it saves your Primary's clip for something that needs the whole clip.

## What it is bad at

- **Volume.** Small clip, modest damage, and Gatekeeper health climbs every Wave of a Run. Deep in a Run it takes visibly more trigger pulls per body than it did on the first Wave, and against a big group it simply cannot keep up.
- **Anything armoured.** It has no pierce, so every shot stops on the first body. A queue of Gatekeepers in a doorway takes one shot each, in order.
- **Rams.** A Ram has far more health than anything else in the game and does not care that you are shooting it. Emptying the sidearm into one repeatedly, with a reload between each clip, is usually slower than the Ram's walk to the Hatch.
- **Being your only weapon past the first couple of Waves.** It is a fine opener and a poor finisher.

## Which Gatekeeper it answers

- **Default** — well enough, early. One at a time, at range, before it reaches the Hatch. Remember that hurting one shouts and pulls its neighbours onto you, so with only the sidearm in hand, do not start a fight you cannot finish.
- **Hunter** — workable, because a Hunter runs straight at you and gets easier to hit the closer it gets, and it is the least healthy thing in the Bastion. It is also faster than your walk, so you will not reliably back away from one.
- **Ram** — no. This is the wrong tool. If a Ram is on the Bastion and the sidearm is all you have, you are trading the Hatch for time.

## Tiering it

The MK-1 has an upgrade ring (strength Reload, weakness Damage). Purchases cost
150 + 150 × current tier on the Secondary curve, and unlock after the first lost Run.

## Gaps

- It is free but must still be bought. A new campaign that never opens the ARMORY fights the entire Bastion with the improvised cutter for no stated reason.
- Its main long-term value is as a swap target during your Primary's reload. Nothing in the game suggests using the Secondary this way.
- Swapping to it mid-reload cancels the Primary's reload progress permanently, so the swap is only free if you were not already reloading.
- Its upgrade wheel is unbuyable, so the ARMORY shows an upgrade the sidearm can never take, with nothing saying it is unfinished rather than unaffordable.
- Falloff starts at 12 m; past maximum range it does nothing, with no visible cue where that line is.
