# Drone Payloads — Coming Soon

## Game

Coming Soon.

The bay was never meant to hold one machine. On the bench, in some order: a mine that arms only under a crowd, and so charges me for letting one form. A welder that fixes a Gatekeeper where it stands, so I can build walls out of the garrison. A breach that opens the hull and drags everything loose toward the hole. An ammo box that moves rounds to where I will be instead of where I was. And a gun that fires during my dead time — the reloads, the walks, the repairs — paying for itself out of my own reserve.

Every one of them will move a fight without carrying it. That is the bay's whole law: finite, sharp, a rescue and never a crutch.
