# The Ante

## Game

The Ante is a wager, placed per Wave, before I commit. It is the one dial on this machine that is mine.

Raising it multiplies what each body pays and how many bodies arrive. Both halves read off the same table and cannot come apart. It is the cleanest mechanism on the Bastion and I do not fully understand why it was built this honest.

It toughens nothing. It tightens nothing. Every extra body is a Default — never a Hunter, never a Thief. A raised Wave is the same fight with more of the cheapest thing in it.

Set it in Prep, at the trigger, before the select. Once Assault begins it is locked. Turning it down is free and refunds nothing.

Three lines of the ledger before I raise it. Can I kill the surplus before it reaches me. What is my health afterwards, given it does not come back while I stand. And is the door already hurt — because repair is billed from the same bank the extra pay lands in. The Ante pays gross. The Hatch charges net.

Start deep and one ante governs the whole chain. I learned that at full price.

## Reference

Look at a field terminal, Master Control, or the Hatch. Anting up raises the Ante — each step spends Unbanked. How much: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md). Select the value, including Ante 0, then start the Wave. Select again unlocks anting up. Without a select the Wave does not start. Bindings: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

Lowering is free. Nothing comes back. Locked during Assault. One ante at Master Control covers the whole sequence.

### What it changes

Raising the Ante multiplies what each Gatekeeper pays, and how many the next Wave sends. Both sides read the same table. Payout and body count cannot come apart.

### What it does not change

- Gatekeepers are not tougher. Health is set by which Wave you are on.
- They do not arrive faster or in bigger groups. Group size and the gap between groups belong to the Wave.
- Every extra body is a Default. The Ante cannot add a Hunter or Thief.

A raised Ante is the same fight with more of the easiest thing in it, and more money per body.

### When you can set it

Only in Prep, looking at the trigger, before select. Anting up refuses after that until select again. Starting a Wave locks it for the whole Assault. That is deliberate: ante up is a pre-commitment, not something you back out of when the room gets loud.

If you start deep from the NAV MAP, the chain is one Assault — ante up before that first start governs every Wave in it.

The prompt prices the next step before you confirm: what a kill pays, how many bodies. Read it. The curve is in the [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

### How to think about raising it

Ask three questions, in this order.

**1. Can you kill the surplus before it reaches you?** Extra bodies hunt you on a field-terminal Wave. Your ceiling is how much you can put out between a group spawning and that group closing. If you are already finishing Waves hurt, more bodies means more chip.

**2. What is your health going to look like afterwards?** Health does not come back while you stand, and the refill that exists is a priced respawn that also eats your Unbanked. A bigger Wave is more swings, and that damage carries into every Wave after it. The Ante's cost is paid across the rest of the Boarding, not just this Wave.

**3. Is the Hatch already hurt?** Repairing costs banked Salvage. If you raise the Ante and the door takes more damage, some of the extra income goes straight back out through the repair. The Ante pays gross; the door charges net.

Anting up spends Unbanked you have not yet taken through a Convert.

Some practical shapes:

- **Early Waves are the cheap place to raise it.** They send the fewest bodies and the softest ones.
- **Late Waves are where a raise actually hurts**, because the surplus lands on top of a Wave that already has the most Hunters and Thieves in it and the toughest bodies.
- **An ante you can't finish is worth nothing.** Losing the Hatch mid-Wave stops the income immediately; extra pay only lands on Gatekeepers you actually kill.
- **Turning it back down is free.** No refund. Nothing commits you to a setting across Waves.

### The one thing the Ante is not

It is not a difficulty setting for the Boarding. It is a per-Wave bet, and the game does not remember it. Every Prep at every trigger is a fresh decision.

## Gaps

- Nothing says the Ante is locked during an Assault. The shortcut panel simply stops showing it, which reads as a bug rather than a rule.
- Nothing warns you that starting deep from the NAV MAP means one ante before that first start governs the entire chained delivery — several Waves' worth of bodies, at a stake you chose before seeing any of them.
- The reward curve is hand-authored with uneven steps. The prompt shows the current and next values but never the shape, so "one more step" is worth a different amount at different points and you cannot see that coming.
- Salvage is floored to a whole number per kill, so part of a raised Ante is quietly rounded away on every body.
- Nothing tells you that the Ante's surplus is entirely Defaults. Without that, raising it reasonably reads as "the Wave gets harder in every way", which is the wrong model and makes the Ante look worse than it is.
