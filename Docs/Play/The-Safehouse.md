# Life Aboard — Coming Soon

## Game

Coming Soon.

The Ship is a hub with four shops in it, and shops are not a life. What is coming is things to do that are not embarking: a bench where a weapon gets calibrated by hand. A board where the crew sees what everyone is bringing before the lead commits us. And work that outlives the session — something done at two in the morning, standing in a friend's world, spent that afternoon by people who know whose it was.

None of it will ever be the efficient path. That rule is already written down. The Ship stays the exhale.

## Reference

From the safehouse brainstorm: activities live on the Ship or behind a door from it; doable prior to or in lieu of core gameplay, never a prerequisite; bonuses stay small; the point is asynchronous contribution with attribution. Pay for an action, never for elapsed time. Nothing is built.
