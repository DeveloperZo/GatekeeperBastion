# Thief

## Game

Heavy frame. The slowest thing on this hull, and unmistakable in a group.

I emptied a clip into one at close range and it did not react. Not a flinch that could be called a flinch. It does not turn. It does not call. The ones walking beside it do not look up.

It has never taken my blood. On a field-terminal Wave it walks at me and takes nothing when it arrives. On a Hatch-bank it wants the Hatch, and it gets there unless I spend the time on it. When it arrives it takes a serious bite.

Time is the entire cost. It is slow, and that slowness is the offer, not a reprieve. Backing away buys seconds. Seconds are not an answer. The only answer is to kill it, and killing it takes time I am spending in front of everything else.

It has the most health of anything here and that scales with the Wave like everything else, so the same frame late in a Run is a substantially longer job than the first one I met. Intensity never adds one.

It is the safe thing to fire into in a crowd, because it is the one target that will not shout the group onto me.

Note: it is built to be shot and appears not to know that it is being shot. I have no explanation for this.

## Reference

### What it does

It walks — slowly, the slowest thing on the Bastion — at you. On a Wave started at a field terminal it does not go to the Hatch, and it never takes your health. On a Hatch-bank it walks to the Hatch and bites that.

Shooting it does not provoke it. It does not speed up, it does not shout, and the Defaults standing beside it stay on their own hunt.

### Nothing shooting does to it

- **Shooting it does not provoke it.** It does not speed up, and it does not slow down.
- **Shooting it alerts nobody.** Unlike every other kind, a Thief taking fire does not shout.
- **It never hears a shout.** Provoking the group around it leaves it on its own hunt.
- **Crowds do not stop it.** It shoulders through its own escort.

It is slow. That is the offer — you have time to deal with a Thief if you spend that time on it. Backing away buys seconds, not a solution. The only answer is to kill it, and killing it takes time you are spending in front of everything else.

### Using it

**It is the safe thing to shoot in a crowd.** If you need to fire into a mass of Gatekeepers, the Thief is the one target that will not shout the group onto you. Everything else you hit will.

**It is the thing to shoot early.** It is slow, so it is behind the group when the group arrives — which is exactly why it gets left until last and then finds you in its face. Its slowness is a budget, not a reprieve.

### Fighting them

It has by far the most health in the game and that health scales up with the Wave like everything else, so the same Thief late in a Run is a substantially longer job than the first one you met. A Wave where the Thieves stack up is a Wave where you should be picking your moment to burn one down rather than trading with chaff.

Intensity never adds Thieves. However high you juice, the number of Thieves is the Wave's, not yours.

## Gaps

- That shooting a Thief does nothing to its mind — no provoke, no shout, no slowdown — is never communicated. It reads like the AI is broken.
- Nothing tells you a Thief is present or how many are left. The Assault count is one number for the whole Wave.
- Its slowness reads as harmlessness. Nothing frames it as a budget you have to spend.
- That it pushes through crowds rather than jamming in them is invisible — you cannot block it with bodies.
