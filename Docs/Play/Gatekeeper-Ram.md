# Ram

The Ram is walking to the Hatch. You are scenery.

Health, damage to the Hatch, speed and how many appear per Wave: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

## What it does

It walks — slowly, the slowest thing on the Bastion — directly to the Hatch, and when it gets there it takes a serious bite out of it with every swing. It hits the Hatch far harder than anything else on the roster.

It cannot hurt you. Not by design intent, but literally: it has no attack against a player and never targets one. You can stand in front of it and it will path around you.

## Nothing you do changes it

This is the part that matters, and it is stronger than it sounds:

- **Shooting it does not provoke it.** It does not turn, it does not speed up, and it does not slow down.
- **Shooting it alerts nobody.** Unlike every other kind, a Ram taking fire does not shout. The Defaults standing beside it stay on their errand.
- **It never hears a shout.** Provoking the group around it leaves it walking.
- **Crowds do not stop it.** It shoulders through its own escort rather than getting stuck behind bodies at the Hatch.

So a Ram is the one threat you cannot solve by moving. Backing away, breaking line of sight, taking better ground, standing somewhere it cannot reach you — every one of those is a real answer to the other two kinds and none of them do anything to a Ram. The clock runs and it keeps walking.

**The only answer is to kill it, and killing it takes time you are spending in front of everything else.** That trade is what the kind is for: the Ram is slow enough that you always have time to deal with it, and dealing with it always costs you the attention that the Hunters escorting it are counting on.

## Using it

Two things follow that are worth exploiting.

**It is the safe thing to shoot in a crowd.** If you need to fire into a mass of Gatekeepers that is not yet coming for you, the Ram is the one target that will not flip the group onto you. Everything else you hit will.

**It is the thing to shoot early.** It is slow, so it is behind the group when the group arrives — which is exactly why players leave it until last and then find it already at the Hatch. Its slowness is a budget, not a reprieve; if you do not spend that time on it, you have spent it.

## Fighting them

It has by far the most health in the game and that health scales up with the Wave like everything else, so the same Ram late in a Run is a substantially longer job than the first one you met. A Wave where the Rams stack up is a Wave where you should be picking your moment to burn one down rather than trading with chaff.

The dial never adds Rams. However high you set it, the number of Rams is the Wave's, not yours.

## Gaps

- That shooting a Ram does nothing at all — no aggro, no shout, no slowdown — is never communicated. It reads like the AI is broken or like it has not noticed you yet.
- That it cannot damage you under any circumstances is never stated. Players retreat from Rams for no reason.
- Nothing tells you a Ram is present or how many are left. The Assault count is one number for the whole Wave.
- The HATCH UNDER ATTACK alarm is the first hard confirmation that a Ram got past you, and by then it has already landed hits.
- Its slowness reads as harmlessness. Nothing frames it as a timer.
- That it pushes through crowds rather than jamming in them is invisible — you cannot block it with bodies or with yourself.
