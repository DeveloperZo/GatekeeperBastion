# Ram

## Game

The Ram is walking to the Hatch — on a Hatch-bank. On a Wave started at a field terminal it walks at you instead. Shooting it does not provoke it. It does not shout, does not speed up, and the Defaults standing beside it stay on their own hunt.

It is the safe thing to fire into in a crowd, and the thing to start on early. Its slowness is a budget, not a reprieve — backing away buys seconds, not a solution.

Health, damage, speed and how many appear per Wave: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

## Reference

### What it does

It walks — slowly, the slowest thing on the Bastion — at you. On a Wave started at a field terminal it does not go to the Hatch.

Shooting it does not provoke it. It does not speed up, it does not shout, and the Defaults standing beside it stay on their own hunt.

### Nothing shooting does to it

- **Shooting it does not provoke it.** It does not speed up, and it does not slow down.
- **Shooting it alerts nobody.** Unlike every other kind, a Ram taking fire does not shout.
- **It never hears a shout.** Provoking the group around it leaves it on its own hunt.
- **Crowds do not stop it.** It shoulders through its own escort.

It is slow. That is the offer — you have time to deal with a Ram if you spend that time on it. Backing away buys seconds, not a solution. The only answer is to kill it, and killing it takes time you are spending in front of everything else.

### Using it

**It is the safe thing to shoot in a crowd.** If you need to fire into a mass of Gatekeepers, the Ram is the one target that will not shout the group onto you. Everything else you hit will.

**It is the thing to shoot early.** It is slow, so it is behind the group when the group arrives — which is exactly why it gets left until last and then finds you in its face. Its slowness is a budget, not a reprieve.

### Fighting them

It has by far the most health in the game and that health scales up with the Wave like everything else, so the same Ram late in a Run is a substantially longer job than the first one you met. A Wave where the Rams stack up is a Wave where you should be picking your moment to burn one down rather than trading with chaff.

Intensity never adds Rams. However high you juice, the number of Rams is the Wave's, not yours.

## Gaps

- That shooting a Ram does nothing to its mind — no provoke, no shout, no slowdown — is never communicated. It reads like the AI is broken.
- Nothing tells you a Ram is present or how many are left. The Assault count is one number for the whole Wave.
- Its slowness reads as harmlessness. Nothing frames it as a budget you have to spend.
- That it pushes through crowds rather than jamming in them is invisible — you cannot block it with bodies.
