# Hunter

The Hunter comes for you and nothing else. It will not hit the Hatch, cannot hit the Hatch, and will not be distracted from you by anything in the Bastion.

Health, damage, speed and how many appear in each Wave: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

## What it does

It picks the nearest living player the moment it arrives and walks straight at them, at full speed, for life. It has no second gear because it never needs one — it is not waiting to be provoked into caring.

It is fast, faster than you can walk away from, and fragile. It dies to very little compared to anything else on the roster. The answer to a Hunter is always to shoot it; there is no positioning answer.

## Why the Hatch is not a shield

A Hunter is not merely uninterested in the Hatch — the Hatch does not exist in its world. It will not attack it while standing on top of it. It will not be pulled toward it by proximity the way a Default is. There is no distance from the Hatch at which a Hunter stops caring about you.

This is precisely why you cannot park in front of the Hatch and hold the trigger down. Standing on the objective is a good answer to Defaults and Rams and no answer at all to a Hunter — it just brings the Hunter to the place you least want a fight, in the middle of the crowd already there.

## Shooting one has a side effect

Hunters arrive hunting, but they have not been *provoked* — which means shooting one still counts as provoking it, and provoking it makes it **shout**. Every Gatekeeper near it joins the hunt.

In practice: opening on a Hunter that is walking in escorted by Defaults converts those Defaults into a group that is now also coming for you. Sometimes that is what you want, since Defaults on you are Defaults not on the Hatch. Often it is not, because you have added bodies to a fight you were trying to end quickly.

Rams in the same group ignore the shout entirely and keep walking.

## Fighting them

Deal with them first and deal with them fast. They are the only kind that threatens your health at speed, they are the cheapest kind to kill, and every second one is alive is a second it spends closing on you regardless of what else you are doing.

The dial never adds Hunters. If a Wave feels like it is drowning you in Hunters specifically, that is the Wave's fixed composition, not your wager — the dial's extra bodies are all Defaults.

## Gaps

- Nothing on screen distinguishes a Hunter from a Default until it changes direction or outruns everything else in the group.
- Its hits and a Default's hits feel identical, so when your health drops you cannot tell which kind is on you without turning around.
- The remaining-Gatekeepers count on the HUD does not separate Hunters out, so you can never confirm you have cleared them.
- That a Hunter cannot damage the Hatch even standing on it is never stated. Players defend the Hatch against them for no reason.
- That shooting a Hunter alerts nearby Defaults is invisible, and it is the most common way to accidentally triple the size of a fight.
- The Hatch alarm is silent on Hunters — no Hatch damage means no alert, so a Hunter arriving is the one threat with no on-screen tell at all.
