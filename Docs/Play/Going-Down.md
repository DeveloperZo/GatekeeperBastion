# Going Down

## Game

Going down is a bill, and I pay it out of my own pocket or the Boarding ends.

The pocket is the Unbanked I am carrying. That is the only thing that can buy me back — not the realm's bank, however fat it is, and not a crewmate's pile however generous they feel. What I killed for is what I stand up on.

The price climbs with every respawn bought this Boarding, whoever it was bought for. The counter is the Boarding's, not mine. One tab, everybody drinking.

What the fee does not take, I keep. I come back holding the rest of it, still uncarried, still mine to lose. That is new and it changes what I do with a fat pile: the pile is not only what I stand to lose, it is also what stands me back up.

Which is the trap in banking. Convert everything at the Hatch and the pot is safe and I am not — I walk back out with empty pockets and the next hit is the last one. Hold too much and I am rich and reckless. Somewhere between the two is the Boarding I actually survive.

Down, I wait behind the vignette with the offer written on it, priced against what I am holding. A guest waits on the Ship with the same offer. If I am not carrying the price and nobody is left standing, there is no offer. That is the whole difference between a setback and a loss.

The Hatch is the exception to everything above. When the door falls there is nothing to buy. The Boarding is over, whatever anyone is carrying.

## Reference

Every dead seat — guest, host, solo — comes back through one rule: a respawn priced from **that seat's own Unbanked**, at a price that climbs per respawn taken this Boarding. The counter belongs to the Boarding and resets with it.

### The price and the purse

Cost is `BaseCost × (respawns taken + 1)` — 150, then 300, then 450 — and it is paid out of the dying seat's carry. **The remainder of that carry survives the death**: the fee is the whole price, and the seat comes back holding what is left.

There is no charge pool and no second window. A mid-Wave return and a return at a break are priced identically, because the carry is the only ration there is: a seat that has been killing can stand up, and a seat that just banked everything cannot.

Nothing else may pay. The realm's bank does not fund returns, and one seat cannot buy another's.

### The surfaces

The Boarder who owns the screen goes down into a DOWN state: a vignette naming the price and the pile it comes out of, or the reason it refuses. Confirm respawns them at the spawn, healed. A guest waits on the Ship; the wait surface and the NAV MAP carry FOLLOW for a seat that never embarked (free — that seat never died) and a priced RESPAWN for one that did, refused by name when the carry is short of the price.

### What a respawn never does

It never touches the realm's bank. It never revives the Hatch: the Hatch at zero ends the Boarding whatever anyone holds. And it never changes the Wave: composition and the wallet split are untouched by a death or a return.

### When there is no offer

A seat carrying less than the price cannot buy back. If no other seat is still fighting, the Boarding ends Lost and that seat's carry dies with it. A Boarder who has banked everything and been hit before earning again is exactly this case — final by arithmetic, not by a special rule.

## Gaps

- Nothing teaches that the price counter is shared across the crew — the first expensive respawn reads as an error rather than a bill run up by earlier deaths.
- That the surviving remainder comes back with you is stated only on the DOWN overlay, and only at the moment you are reading it under pressure.
- Nothing warns a Boarder walking away from a full convert that they are now unable to buy a death back until they earn again.
