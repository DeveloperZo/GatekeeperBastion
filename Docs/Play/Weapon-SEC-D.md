# SEC-D

Your **close-quarters Secondary**. Free at the ARMORY. Codename until identity is solid (`../Decisions.md`). The other free Secondary is the **MK-1** (reach).

Numbers: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

## What it is for

**Contact when the Primary cannot.** Empties its regenerating pool in under a second, hits hard inside a few metres, and does nothing across a room. Paired with the MR-35 it answers a Hunter in your face; paired with the BR-12 it commits the whole loadout to close range.

## How it wants to be used

- Swap to it when something is already on you, not when something is still walking down the corridor.
- Empty the pool into the body in front of you, then let regen refill during the quiet — it is not a thrifty gun.
- Do not bother aiming it at the far Hatch bay; the MK-1 owns that job.

## What it is bad at

- **Reach.** Past ~8 m it is a dead trigger. At the MK-1's working distance it does near-zero.
- **Sustain.** The clip is gone in a fraction of a second; regen is slow relative to dump rate.
- **Rams.** Same class problem as the MK-1 — wrong tool for armour.
