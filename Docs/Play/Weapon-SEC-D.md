## Game

The contact Secondary. Free at the ARMORY. The other free Secondary is the MK-1, which trades contact for reach.

It dumps a regenerating pool into whatever is already touching me. Inside a few metres it hits hard. At the distance the MK-1 works comfortably it is a dead trigger.

Swap to it when something is on me, not when something is still walking down the corridor. Empty the pool into the body in front of me and let it refill during the quiet. The refill is slow relative to how fast it empties.

Paired with a reach Primary it is the answer to a Hunter in my face. Paired with the BR-12 it commits the whole loadout to knife range, which is a decision about the entire Run rather than about this gun.

Wrong tool for a Thief.

It carries no ability, and that spoke on its wheel cannot be bought. I do not know why it is drawn there.

## Reference

### What it is for

Contact when the Primary cannot. It dumps its regenerating pool in a burst, hits hard
inside a few metres, and does nothing across a room. Paired with the MR-35 it answers a
Hunter in your face. Paired with the BR-12 it commits the whole loadout to close range.

### How it wants to be used

Swap to it when something is already on you, not when something is still walking down the
corridor. Empty the pool into the body in front of you, then let regen refill during the
quiet. Do not bother aiming it at the far Hatch bay; the MK-1 owns that job.

### What it is bad at

**Reach.** At the MK-1's working distance it is a dead trigger. **Sustain.** The clip is
gone in a fraction of a second; regen is slow relative to dump rate. **Thieves.** Wrong tool
for armour.

### Tiering it

Ring is FireRate–Ability–Reload–Clip–Damage, strength FireRate, weakness Reload. Ability
cannot be bought (`AbilityId.None`); the other four spokes can, on the Secondary cost
curve, after the first lost Run.
