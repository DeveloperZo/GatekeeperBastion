# Salvage

## Game

Salvage is the only currency. There is no second resource, and nothing here to collect but the pay.

The sequence never changes: kills fill Unbanked, a Hatch-bank converts it, converted lands with the realm. Prep pays nothing. Walking pays nothing. Surviving pays nothing. Stripping the place pays no bonus. Killing, during a live Wave. That is the entire payroll.

Unbanked is wiped if I go down or the Hatch falls. Converted stays, whatever happens after. A Run that fell apart early paid exactly what made it through, and not one unit more.

Two things move the money backwards: repairing, and standing dead crew back up. Both bill the bank I shop with — repair as the healing lands, respawns at a price that climbs per death this Run. A Run where the door takes a beating costs twice: once at the door, once at the console I walk back to and cannot afford. A dying crew runs the same arithmetic faster.

A Run holds a limited count of Hatch-banks. When they are spent, the Hatch refuses, politely and permanently.

Everything competes for one pile — weapons, Tiers, Hatch Armor, the Rig, the Decoy, the repairs I did not plan for, the deaths I did not plan for. The bank is not a score. It is not banked so I can look at it.

## Reference

Salvage is the only currency in the game. There is nothing else to collect, no second resource, no crafting material. Everything you will ever buy is bought with this.

### How you earn it

**By killing Gatekeepers, and by nothing else — until you Hatch-bank.** A kill fills Unbanked. Prep select then start at the Hatch starts a Hatch-bank Wave: drip while it is live, dump on clear. That convert is what credits the ship. The quiet of Prep earns nothing. Walking the room earns nothing. Clearing the Bastion pays no completion bonus.

What one body is worth is a flat kill payout multiplied by wherever you left intensity. Those two multiply against each other. More field Waves before the Hatch-bank convert at a higher multiplier, and the Hatch-bank fight is the Wave equal to how many you carried.

### Why it is at risk

**A Gatekeeper's Salvage sits Unbanked until a Hatch-bank.** Not in your shop purse the moment it drops. Down, or Hatch to zero, wipes what you have not converted. What already dripped or dumped stays on the ship.

A Run has a limited number of Hatch-banks. How many: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md). When they are gone, looking at the Hatch and starting refuses. Juice also spends Unbanked, so a raise you cannot convert is a raise you never banked.

This is why leaving the Hatch is an economy, not just a door. Being Lost keeps converted Salvage. Losing the Hatch keeps converted Salvage. There is no named forfeit. A Run that fell apart early paid you only for what made it through a Hatch-bank.

A Run you convert through a Hatch-bank and a Run you lose differ in whether leftover Unbanked survives. Converted Salvage stays either way. Wave 5 does not convert leftover and does not wipe it. See [A Run](A-Run.md).

### The two things that take it back

**Repairing the Hatch.** Repair is charged out of your banked Salvage as the healing lands — the same bank you were saving, spent from inside the Run. See [The Hatch](The-Hatch.md).

**Respawns.** A dead seat buys its way back from the same bank, at a price that climbs with every respawn taken this Run. See [Going Down](Going-Down.md).

This is the one place where the game can move your money in the wrong direction, and it is worth understanding as a cost rather than an action. **A Run where the Hatch takes a beating is a Run that costs you twice:** once in the Salvage that went into the door, and again in the purchase you walked back to the Ship unable to make.

### What competes for it

Everything wants the same pile:

- **A weapon** at the ARMORY. The largest single decision, and the one that fixes what kind of fight you are good at.
- **Weapon Tiers**, also at the ARMORY, per weapon. The deepest sink in the game and the least transferable — the money goes into one gun and stays there.
- **The Hatch** at FABRICATION: a bigger pool (Hatch Armor), the Repair Rig that makes repairing possible at all, and the Decoy.
- **Repairs, mid-Run.** The bill you did not plan for.
- **Respawns, mid-Run.** The other one, and it climbs.

CREW QUARTERS spends nothing on Default (cost 0, same as the free sidearm). VEIL and
FORGE are claimable there too.

There is no order that is correct for everyone, but there is a wrong instinct: treating the bank as a score. It is not banked so you can look at it. See [Upgrades](Upgrades.md) for what to do with it.

## Gaps

- The HUD shows Unbanked and Banked separately. Kills move Unbanked; Banked moves during a Hatch-bank.
- It never tells you that a Gatekeeper killed outside a live Wave pays nothing.
- Every station panel shows lifetime earned and lifetime spent. Neither is explained anywhere, neither is a resource, and neither can be used for anything.
- If a save is old or damaged, those lifetime figures are silently rebuilt from what you own. Your bank is always the truth; the lifetime lines may be a reconstruction.
- There is no way to sell anything back or refund a purchase, and nothing tells you that before you spend.
- The Hatch-bank slot count is in the Field Manual. Nothing on the Hatch names how many you have left until a start is refused.
