# Default Protocol

## Game

The sheet everything else is measured against. It costs nothing and it is claimable the first minute I stand on my Ship.

Its doctrine is that there is no doctrine. Hardware behaves exactly as FABRICATION sold it. Nothing I place is quietly different from the panel's description, and I have learned to price that honesty.

The crew burst is a plant. I put it down, I am rooted while it runs, and my gun and every Boarder standing near me dump fire for the window. I can pull it up early. The root is the entire price: the fastest I will ever shoot, from a spot I chose before the group arrived — or a spot I will die in, because I chose late.

The personal burst keeps me moving and speeds only my own gun. Less, for a shorter window, on a longer wait — but on my feet.

No passive. Nothing standing, nothing conditional, nothing to remember at the wrong moment.

It is not the beginner sheet. It is the sheet that adds output and moves nothing else. The room keeps looking wherever it was looking. It just runs out of bodies sooner.

## Reference

**Cost: nothing**, and it is still claimed at CREW QUARTERS, for the same reason the first Secondary is sold rather than given.

### Doctrine: STOCK

Hardware as sold. A decoy placed while wearing Default behaves exactly as FABRICATION described it, with the payload's own authored live count.

### Crew: Barrage

Planted and rooted for the window. Your fire rate and that of other Boarders inside the radius are multiplied, and they are not rooted. Pressing again, or the window ending, unplants you.

The output belongs to the crew, the root belongs to you. Solo, the crew half degrades to you alone and the root does not.

Values and bindings: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md).

### Personal: Rush

Your gun only, and you keep moving. A smaller multiplier than Barrage's, for a shorter window, on a longer cooldown.

### Passive: none

Default is the only sheet without one. Nothing standing, nothing conditional.

### How it plays

- **Barrage is a Prep decision, not an Assault reaction.** You are choosing where to be rooted before the group arrives. Plant on ground with cover and a short approach, not in the open because a group is already on you.
- **Rush is the answer when Barrage's root would kill you.** It is worth less on purpose. The choice between them is mobility, not damage.
- **Barrage does not care who else is in the radius,** so in co-op the plant is worth more the closer the crew is fighting. Solo it is a personal window with a root attached, and it is still the highest output on the sheet.
- **Nothing on this sheet moves attention.** Everything that was coming for you is still coming for you, faster than you can kill it or not.

## Gaps

- The root on Barrage is not signposted before you press it. You learn it by being unable to move with a Hunter closing.
- Nothing shows the crew radius, so you cannot tell whether a teammate is inside it.
- Rush and Barrage are on separate cooldowns and the HUD does not make the two clocks comparable at a glance.
- That STOCK is a real doctrine rather than the absence of one is never stated. It reads as an empty row.
