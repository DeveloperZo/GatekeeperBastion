## Ancestor (public)

[Guns 'n Goblins](https://store.steampowered.com/app/881940/Guns_n_Goblins/)
(Steam app 881940). [Demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4671750/Guns_n_Goblins_Demo/)
on Steam (press: 2026-07-24). Full game **Q2 2027**. Genre on Steam: Action, Indie, RPG.
Visible user-tag chips are JS-hidden on the store HTML without a session.
[Steamlikes](https://steamlikes.co/app/881940/Guns-n-Goblins) (refreshed this week)
shows **FPS, Action, RPG, Survival, Idler** as the first five. Incremental remains
the **official short description**. Building / Dark Fantasy / Incremental-as-a-chip:
unverified this pass.

Steam blurb, unchanged on fetch 2026-08-30: "incremental horde survivor FPS. Build your
Bastion, level up your character, and upgrade your arsenal to rise from Apprentice Bell
Ringer to Master Dragon Hunter."

**Demo reception (new since the last stamp).** Fetched 2026-08-30 from app 4671750:
**Overwhelmingly Positive**, 97% of **931** All Reviews; Recent 97% of 556. The deluge
fantasy has public proof it sells. Copy that shares their verbs now has a reviewed
demo to be filed against, not only a wishlist page.

Press files it the same way:

- GameShed: castle defense + incremental progression; thousands on screen;
  ring the bell to summon more
  ([gameshedge](https://www.gameshedge.com/horde-survival-fps-shooter-guns-n-goblins-pc-announced/))
- FullCleared: "roguelite" / survivor-like; designer quote *why isn't there an
  incremental FPS built around massive hordes*
  ([fullcleared](https://fullcleared.com/news/guns-n-goblins-looks-to-be-our-next-roguelite-obsession/))
- GamingOnLinux: "first-person bullet heaven"
  ([gamingonlinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/05/blow-up-thousands-of-monsters-in-the-horde-based-survivor-fps-guns-n-goblins/))
- GamingBible (2026-07-27, demo): "Think of it like a 3D version of Vampire Survivors"
  ([gamingbible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/platform/steam/steam-july-demo-elder-scrolls-meets-doom-game-220579-20260727))

What **this** project kept (Design, not Steam): you call the swarm; a lost Boarding
still pays. What it inverted: Bastion is **theirs**; you board. What it refused:
idler, incremental hero sheet, build-your-fortress, thousands-strong spectacle,
single-player as the only session (`../Design/1-Experience.md` §1.1 ancestor).
The "call the swarm" half is **parked** in 0.34.2 (`Ante.Enabled = false`) — you
still start every Wave; you do not dial it.

**Reception risk:** any copy that shares their verbs (deluge, rebuild, XP,
thousands, "your Bastion") will be reviewed as a GnG clone. The shared DNA is
real; the shelf is fatal if we land on Idler / Incremental / Bullet Heaven. The
demo's Overwhelmingly Positive score raises the cost of that filing: reviewers
now have a live object to compare against.

## Comparisons we can afford

**Say (when friends-alpha is real):** you and a friend board a garrison, you
start every fight, you leave with what banked as it fell.

**Do not say:** Vampire Survivors with a mouse; GnG in space; Left 4 Dead with
a siphon; a tower-defense FPS; Arc Raiders without PvP; Marathon without PvP;
Helldivers but first-person; Space Marine 2 Siege; an extraction shooter.

## Accurate vs failed impression

**Accurate:** sci-fi FPS; you are the Boarder; they hold the Bastion; quiet
until you start at the Hatch; banked Salvage survives a loss; a friend is the
aim of the roadmap.

**Failed:** incremental; idler; bullet heaven; castle defense; thousands on
screen; you rebuild **your** Bastion; AEGIS or LEDGER in the trailer (VEIL and
FORGE ship and may appear); plural destinations; Ante as a live dial; Salvage
waiting on a Convert; extraction shooter; extract / exfil / “get in, get paid,
get out”; siege / defend the fortress; unlock skills / ultimate arsenal;
two-deck Ship.
