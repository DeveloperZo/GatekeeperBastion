These are how a Boarding feels. None of them needs new content. A player who never
reads them as a story has lost nothing.

## 1. The work is clean

**In the chair.** You know the job by the end of the first Prep. Cut in, hold the
door, walk the lane, throw the switch, go home. Nobody explains anything because
nothing needs explaining. You are not a chosen anything. You are somebody who does
this.

The first time you dock with a full bank you understand the whole game.

**Carrier.** `../Play Mechanics/Getting-Started.md`, which is pure sequence and reads
as one person explaining a job to the next person who will do it. The Situation and
A Boarding volumes generally.

**For.** Establish that returning is not a topic. The early Boardings teach embark
and dock, and nobody frames that as strange.

## 2. The kit is the identity

**In the chair.** You pick a weapon and a protocol on the Ship, then you fight in
what you brought. A protocol changes what the hardware does without changing what
the hardware is. That is the whole identity of a Boarding — closer to "this is the
most this gun has ever been" than to a class screen.

You do not roll a random Boarder. Random start would delete the reason you opened
CREW QUARTERS.

**Carrier.** `../Play Mechanics/Weapons.md`, `../Play Mechanics/Upgrades.md`,
`../Play Mechanics/Protocols.md`, the three shipped sheets. FORGE's Outrider is kit,
not a companion (`../_meta/_definitions/language.md`).

**For.** Expression is the session's face. Shared rules across kit and garrison read
as clean design. They are allowed to stay that.

## 3. The fight has a direction

**In the chair.** You press `T` at the Hatch. The wave walks you up their ship. You
throw `T` at the zone's terminal to shut it off. Killing the field is not finishing;
arriving is. The Hatch can die behind you while you are on the lane. After a zone
you may Dock, or recall and start the next one.

Master Control is the last zone. An elite arms the switch; throwing it is BASTION
STRIPPED. It is not a Convert window, and it is not a revelation.

**Carrier.** The loop in `../Design/0-Gameplay.md`. Hatch portal and recall pads as
built. `../Play Mechanics/Master-Control.md`.

**For.** This is the Howling Abyss move: one lane, a place around it, a session that
ends at a switch. The floor plan is why it is not a void with a bridge on it.

## 4. They keep coming

**In the chair.** You have killed everything in this stretch several times and the
Bastion does not care. They come in groups, from doors you cannot open, at a rate
nothing you do changes. Clearing a Wave clears a Wave. The trickle keeps the lane
honest until the terminal is thrown.

**Carrier.** `../Play Mechanics/Gatekeepers.md` — the hand admits it does not know
where they come from and that the rooms do not open for it. That line is job notes,
not a riddle.

**For.** The mode. Minions keep walking the lane in ARAM for the same reason.

## 5. The Ship keeps the pot

**In the chair.** You walk the Ship and the record is bigger than this Boarding.
Banked Salvage, bastion progress, deepest Wave. It belongs to the place. After a
Dock or a loss you are back in the lobby with the kit still yours and the bank still
there. That is the one-more-Boarding lever.

**Carrier.** The realms model as built. The end screen's lifetime counters.

**For.** Soft failure. The persistent thing is the place. You are the part that
gets replaced, and nobody comments on that.

## The hands

**In the chair.** You are reading the Compendium for a mechanical answer and the
writing feels different from the last page. One entry counts and sequences. Another
is preoccupied with who is looking at you. A third talks about the room like a
load-bearing structure.

Most players register this as personality. Some register it as people. Both are
fine.

**Carrier.** The whole Compendium. One hand per shipped protocol, unnamed, undated,
unsigned. See [Voices](Voices.md).

**For.** Flavor. Not evidence that there has been more than one of you, and not a
beat the game aims at.

## Never a beat

**Recognition.** The reading that you are the process is wallpaper (`Premise.md`).
No scene, no music sting, no line, no carrier. The game is equally good if no one
ever has it.

**Break the process.** Beat 7's second exit does not exist. Escaped and Docked are
the session ends. Docked is leaving with what you moved; the realm keeps every
counter. Do not author a spend-the-realm ending from this folder. See
[Open-Rulings](Open-Rulings.md).
