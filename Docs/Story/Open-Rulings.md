## Open

### The break ending

Beat 7's second exit does not exist. Escaped is the only terminal good ending in the build.

Recommendation: do not author it yet. Beats 1 through 6 cost almost nothing because they ride
on systems already built or already being written. The second exit needs an end state, a
screen, a save consequence, and a decision about whether a realm can be spent. That is a
milestone family, and putting it in front of the co-op demo trades a shippable arc for an
unshippable one. Ship through beat 6, where Escaped is the only ending and reads as
repetition to anyone who noticed. Author the break when ending work comes up on its own
merits.

### Co-op and the premise

Several Boarders stand on one Ship in one realm at the same time. Under this premise that is
either a hole or the loudest evidence in the game.

Recommendation: the latter, and nothing is said about it by anyone. A downed guest already
buys back from the realm's pot and rejoins at the Hatch, which is the process visibly paying
to put a version of a Boarder back in the room. Do not comment on it, do not have a Boarder
react to it, and let it stay a mechanic that happens to be the thesis.

## Assumed, pending correction

Each of these was assumed to lay the foundation, and each is cheap to reverse.

**On the world**

- **The Bastion is infrastructure with an owner, and the owner is never named.** No faction,
  no empire, no war. The garrison is a standing order, not a grudge.
- **Salvage is what a Gatekeeper is made of.** It becomes money by travelling the tether,
  which is why Unbanked dies with you.
- **The realm is the continuity and the Boarder is the replaceable part.** This is the
  fiction of the persistence split as built, not a new idea.
- **The Boarder has no name, face, history or lines.** Anything that would characterise the
  Boarder is out, including a mirror moment or a reflection.
- **The source is never named in the game.** It appears only as two rosters obeying the same
  rules.

**On delivery**

- **Three hands now, one per shipped protocol.** Default, VEIL and FORGE. AEGIS and LEDGER
  are not sold, so they have no hand.
- **The hands never contradict each other.** Two lenses on one subject already disagree
  without either being wrong. An explicit correction between hands was considered and cut:
  it reads as a documentation bug first.
- **No Hatch line yet.** The budget allows one repeated line at the Hatch if a beat cannot
  land without it. No beat currently needs one, so none is written.
- **Field Manual links are struck from the Game blocks.** They pointed at a numbers appendix
  from a voice that no longer carries numbers. The page stays a builder artifact.
- **Recognition is not gated, tracked or rewarded.** No achievement, no flag, no check.
- **The story does not touch the save schema.** Nothing in beats 1 through 6 needs a new
  persisted field.
