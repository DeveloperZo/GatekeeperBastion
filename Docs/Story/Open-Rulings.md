## Open

### The break ending

A second exit that spends the realm still does not exist. Escaped (BASTION STRIPPED,
Master terminal thrown after the elite arms it) and Docked (portal or Hatch after a
zone close) are the session ends. Docked is the loop continuing.

Recommendation: do not author it. A spend-the-record ending makes loss feel like
grief, which the premise forbids, and it turns the backdrop into a plot. Author it
only if ending work comes up on its own merits and the unique lane in
[README](README.md) has been abandoned on purpose.

### Mid-Boarding remix

ARAM: Mayhem's steal is offer-three-pick-one after the fight has already started —
remix the kit you brought, not a random Boarder at embark. That is a mechanic, not a
story beat. Cousins already live in `../Brainstorms/boarder-protocols.md` (the
modifier pool) and `../Brainstorms/coop-bastion-complexity.md` (a draft at the off
verb). Riot's own failure modes: trait-set homogenization, unreadability past four
stacked modifiers, death-gated power, and a champion-specific catalog that had to
prune 450+ dead draws.

Recommendation: do not start that catalog from a story pass. If it graduates, it
graduates from those brainstorms, with a kill criterion, and it must not randomize
the loadout, the start, or the stake. The Ante stays a bet you place when it is on.

### Co-op and the premise

Several Boarders stand on one Ship in one realm at the same time. That is the loudest
evidence in the game and nothing is said about it.

A downed seat buys back from the **realm's bank** (`RunSim.TryRespawn` →
`SpendBanked`), cap 2 this Boarding, bleed-out 10s. The carry is not the purse. Do
not comment on replacement, do not have a Boarder react to it, and let it stay a
mechanic.

## Assumed, pending correction

Each of these was assumed to lay the foundation, and each is cheap to reverse.

**On the world**

- **The Bastion is infrastructure with an owner, and the owner is never named.** No
  faction, no empire, no war. The garrison is a standing order, not a grudge.
- **Salvage is what a Gatekeeper is made of.** A kill banks as it lands. Unbanked is
  only Withdraw. Respawn spends the bank.
- **The realm is the continuity and the Boarder is the replaceable part.** This is
  the fiction of the persistence split as built, not a new idea.
- **The Boarder has no name, face, history or lines.** Anything that would
  characterise the Boarder is out, including a mirror moment or a reflection.
- **The source is never named in the game.** It appears only as two rosters obeying
  the same rules, and that symmetry is not a clue the game is hunting.

**On delivery**

- **Three hands now, one per shipped protocol.** Default, VEIL and FORGE. AEGIS and
  LEDGER are not sold, so they have no hand.
- **The hands never contradict each other.** Two lenses on one subject already
  disagree without either being wrong. An explicit correction between hands was
  considered and cut: it reads as a documentation bug first.
- **No Hatch line yet.** The budget allows one repeated line at the Hatch if a beat
  cannot land without it. No beat currently needs one, so none is written.
- **Field Manual links are struck from the Game blocks.** They pointed at a numbers
  appendix from a voice that no longer carries numbers. The page stays a builder
  artifact.
- **Recognition is not gated, tracked or rewarded.** No achievement, no flag, no
  check.
- **The story does not touch the save schema.** Nothing in the session beats needs a
  new persisted field.
- **The Ante is parked.** Player-facing ante-up is off; Waves run at dial 0. The
  wager language in this folder is heading, not a live readout.
- **Demo PA / named guides stay furniture.** `../Demo - Single/voice.md` may carry a
  line. They do not explain return, source, or thesis. `M62` already cut a companion
  narrator.
