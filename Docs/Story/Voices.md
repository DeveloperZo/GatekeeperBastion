The player-facing dimension of every Compendium entry, the `## Game` block, is a Boarder's
working notes. Not authorial voice. It reports what was observed and never quotes a rule.

This is the whole story budget in practice. Almost everything a player will ever feel about
the world, they will feel here, while looking up something mechanical. The hands are flavor.
They are not evidence the game is collecting toward a reveal. Authoring lives in
`../Play Mechanics/`; the in-game reader copies it.

## One hand per protocol

There is one writing hand per shipped Boarder Protocol. **It is never revealed explicitly.**
No names, no dates, no signatures, no protocol names in the text. A reader who never notices
there is more than one author has lost nothing. A reader who does has found wallpaper.

The tell is the lens. What a hand notices is what its doctrine makes salient.

| Hand | Lens | Entries |
|---|---|---|
| STOCK | sequence, counts, what happened in what order | The Game, First Time Out, A Boarding, Waves, Salvage, The Ship, Protocols, Protocol-Default, Gatekeeper-Default, MK-1, BR-12, Realms, Shared Realms *(CS)*, The Other Currencies *(CS)*, Destinations *(CS)*, Life Aboard *(CS)* |
| PHANTOM | attention, who is looking at whom, being noticed | Gatekeepers, Hunter, Cloaker, Staying Alive, SEC-D, Protocol-VEIL, The Crew, and the eight unshipped garrison rows *(all CS)* |
| PARALLEL BAYS | load, throughput, what a thing is made of and what it takes | The Hatch, The Ante, Thief, Weapons, MR-35, PRI-C, The Drone, Upgrades, Protocol-FORGE, Going Down, AEGIS *(CS)*, LEDGER *(CS)*, Drone Payloads *(CS)* |

Assignment is by relevance, not rotation, so the set reads as three specialists before it
reads as three people. Each protocol sheet is written by its own hand — the one place the
authorship comes closest to the surface, and it still is not stated.

**Growth rule.** A newly shipped protocol brings a hand and writes new entries only. Existing
entries keep their hand permanently and are never reassigned, or the Compendium churns on
every roster addition. The roster target is 8 to 10 protocols, so the hand set grows the same
way. A protocol without a natural lens writes nothing rather than getting a manufactured one.

**Coming Soon rule.** A page marked Coming Soon (*CS* above) carries the marker in its
title, stays in the build so the nav shows the roster as unfinished on purpose, and is
written by the nearest *existing* hand — the protocol it marks has no hand until it ships.
It is short: a couple of true sentences about what the thing will be about, never a
mechanic described as live. When the thing ships, the page is replaced, not edited, and
the replacing hand is whichever the grown roster assigns.

## Tone

Concise, analytical, first person, and each hand owns a register — a cadence, an obsession
and an attitude, not just a lens:

- **STOCK** is the quartermaster: flat declaratives, lists kept in order, procedure as
  comfort, dry fatalism. *"I sort them the way you sort anything walking at you: by shape,
  at distance, early."*
- **PHANTOM** is the stalker: unsettled and unsettling, rooms as things with eyes,
  comfortable saying what it cannot prove. *"I know it is in the room the way you know a
  door you closed is standing open."*
- **PARALLEL BAYS** is the engineer: everything is an invoice, respect for well-built
  machines including the enemy's, gallows arithmetic. *"Its slowness is not mercy. It is
  the invoice. It is armor with an appointment."*

The awe is the fantasy, and it is allowed to breathe (recut 2026-08-24, Zo — the earlier
four-words-of-wonder ration produced flat pages and is retired; the lines quoted above are
the bar). A hand notices something precisely and builds one image with teeth out of it,
then moves on. What stays banned is decoration: adjectives doing an observation's work,
and gushing. The test of an image is whether it carries a rule — an image that could
caption any game has failed; one that teaches this room's arithmetic has not.

## What gets obscured

**Accuracy, not truth.** Never a figure, a rate, or a key name. Say "often", not a percentage.
Imprecise is allowed. False is not.

Use the ladder so a word means the same thing on every page:

- Frequency: never, rarely, sometimes, often, usually, always.
- Magnitude: comparative only. The slowest thing here. The most health of anything. The
  shortest reach I have carried.
- Time: a moment, seconds, long enough to cross the room, longer than you think.

**Contract facts stay flat.** Anything the player must act on or lose the Run is stated
plainly and without decoration: health does not return, zero on the Hatch ends it, Unbanked
is gone, repair spends the bank and needs the Rig, the loadout is fixed at embark. Mechanism
is what gets obscured. Consequence never is. Obscuring a consequence is not mystery, it is a
trap, and it costs the trust the wager depends on.

**Reference is withheld from the build.** The `## Reference` block stays a builder artifact.
Some rules are meant to be experienced rather than read, and there is no dimension toggle in
the reader.

## The lint

Every sentence in a Game block must be derivable from a sentence in that page's Reference
section. No new claim, no softened claim, and no claim that fails when a player tests it.
This is what separates obscuring from lying, and it is checkable by eye.
