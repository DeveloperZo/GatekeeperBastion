What we have worked out about the place we cut into, the garrison that already holds it,
the hull we leave from, the worlds we hold, and why the trip is worth taking. The entries
point at each other because the answers do. A page marked Coming Soon names something on
its way; it is short on purpose.

## I. The Situation

I am the Boarder. The Bastion is held. The Hatch is the way home.

[First Time Out](Getting-Started.md) is the first Bastion in order, from a dark Ship to
stripped — the pause reader opens there and shows the numbered steps. [The Game](The-Game.md)
is the shape of the whole thing: what a Boarding is, why there are kinds, what losing costs.

## II. A Boarding

One trip out and back. [A Boarding](A-Boarding.md) is embark, Prep, Assault, Convert,
and the four ways it finishes. [Waves](Waves.md) is how the room gets worse.
[The Ante](Ante.md) is parked: the dial is off and Waves run at zero.
[Salvage](Salvage.md) is the only currency, banked the moment a body falls —
[The Other Currencies](Experience-And-Cores.md), Coming Soon, are the two that will join it.
[Going Down](Going-Down.md) is what a death costs and who pays it.
[Staying Alive](Staying-Alive.md) is what the room rewards, traced back to the rule that
makes each one work.

## III. The Bastion

The place I hold. [The Hatch](The-Hatch.md) is the umbilical: the only defended thing in the
Boarding, the bill I did not plan for, and the way home after a survived Convert.
[Destinations](Destinations.md), Coming Soon, is the chart of other places to cut into.

## IV. The Garrison

They live here. The roster runs to twelve frames; four walk today, and each one is defined
by what it refuses. [Gatekeepers](Gatekeepers.md) is the garrison whole: how they arrive,
what puts one on me, how Waves mix them.

Then the kinds: [Default](Gatekeeper-Default.md), the only one I can steer.
[Hunter](Gatekeeper-Hunter.md), which wants me and never the Hatch.
[Thief](Gatekeeper-Thief.md), which will not turn however much I shoot it.
[Cloaker](Gatekeeper-Cloaker.md), which moves only where nobody is looking.

And eight more still in their rooms, each Coming Soon, known for now only by what it will
take — they get names when they walk: [the fifth frame](Gatekeeper-Source.md) the empty
room, [the sixth](Gatekeeper-Enabler.md) the other frames' jobs,
[the seventh](Gatekeeper-Alarmer.md) the group's size, [the eighth](Gatekeeper-Bypasser.md)
coverage, [the ninth](Gatekeeper-Reclaimer.md) what is left where something died,
[the tenth](Gatekeeper-Exploder.md) the ground I stand on,
[the eleventh](Gatekeeper-Standoff-Shooter.md) the Hatch from range, and
[the twelfth](Gatekeeper-Ailment-Shooter.md), which takes something that is not blood.

## V. Doctrine

The protocol I embark wearing. [Protocols](Protocols.md) is the system: the four rows every
sheet answers, where it is claimed, and what a protocol never touches.

Then the sheets: [Default](Protocol-Default.md), which adds output and changes nothing else.
[VEIL](Protocol-VEIL.md), which moves attention onto me and then takes me off the list.
[FORGE](Protocol-FORGE.md), which builds the thing they look at.

The roster is not finished: [AEGIS](Protocol-AEGIS.md) and [LEDGER](Protocol-LEDGER.md)
are Coming Soon.

## VI. Arms

Every decision about the gun in my hands was made on the Ship. [Weapons](Weapons.md) is the
doctrine: two slots, buying, the wheel, and how the three Primaries map onto the three
refusals.

Then the guns: [MK-1 Sidearm](Weapon-MK-1-Sidearm.md), [SEC-D](Weapon-SEC-D.md),
[BR-12 Shotgun](Weapon-BR-12-Shotgun.md), [MR-35 Rifle](Weapon-MR-35-Rifle.md),
[PRI-C](Weapon-PRI-C.md).

And the slot that is not a gun: [The Drone](The-Drone.md) is the bay and the Decoy that
rides in it today; [Drone Payloads](Drone-Payloads.md), Coming Soon, is the rest of the
bench.

## VII. The Hull

Between Boardings I walk my own Ship. [The Ship](The-Ship.md) is the hub and the four consoles,
each owning one subject. [Upgrades](Upgrades.md) is what is permanent, what waits for the
first Salvage earned, and what is actually worth buying.
[Life Aboard](The-Safehouse.md), Coming Soon, is what there will be to do here besides
leaving.

## VIII. The Realm

The world is a thing I keep, and I am a thing that travels between worlds.
[Realms](Realms.md) is the split: what stays with the world, what leaves with me, and
whose money the pot is. [The Crew](The-Crew.md) is playing a world together: the join
code, following in at the break, and a room that counts us.
[Shared Realms](Shared-Realms.md) is Coming Soon: a friend's world that answers when they
are not home.

## Appendix

The numbers live in one place: the [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md), generated from the game itself — every
figure, key, cost and Wave table. The entries above say "often" and "the slowest thing
here" on purpose; when a page owes you a number it points there. The pause reader does
not open it; it ships beside the Compendium for reading outside the game.
