# The Faucet Loop

The Faucet Loop is the shape of a field fight: a Wave begins at the Hatch and ends at the
zone's terminal, so the objective is a place to walk to rather than a body count. The
Boarder decides when to leave the Hatch and when to end the Wave. The trip out is the leg
that is paid for: reaching the switch banks the whole carried pile, and the lane keeps
dripping Gatekeepers that pay nothing, so it cannot be cleared in advance from the safety of
the door. That shape is in the build and every player meets it. A set of extensions on top
of it is built, sits behind a developer-only switch, and has never been played. Those
extensions: Waves that keep coming on their own, Gatekeepers that march on the Hatch instead
of the Boarder, income for Waves endured, and a choice of modifiers that grows the longer
the Boarder holds.

## The interaction

The Boarder invokes it against the Boarding's Wave state, at the two ends of a zone's lane. **The mechanic is in the build and on by default**: its spine is THE LOOP (Zo, 2026-08-25, enforced 2026-08-26) — *a wave begins at the Hatch and ends at a terminal* — and it is the loop every player meets today. What is not on is the set of extensions M102 grayboxed on top of it.

**In build.** `T` at the Hatch runs the current zone's Wave span; the Hatch is the only start line, and a field terminal refuses to start a Wave while it is (`CanSelectTerminal`). `T` at that zone's terminal shuts the Wave off — in Assault **or** in Prep, because the field may already be empty when the Boarder gets there and the walk is still the objective (Zo, 2026-08-26). The Wave does not have to be killed to the last body.

The shutoff banks the whole carried pile, completes the terminal outright, opens the zone's door and offers the dock. Between the two ends the lane keeps dripping: the field trickle holds the walk open once the authored roster is spent, and pays nothing. [A Boarding](A-Boarding.md) owns the Boarding that contains this shape and describes it in full — the composite span, the door, the enders — and this page does not restate it.

**What the shape is for.** The identity of this mechanic is that **the terminal is an off switch, not a trigger**, and that makes the objective positional rather than attritional: the Wave ends where the Boarder walks to, so the spine between the Hatch and the terminal is the fight rather than the corridor after it (distances: [Field Manual](Field-Manual.md)). The pricing follows from that in one asymmetry — **the trip out is the priced leg**. Reaching the switch *is* the payout (`ShutOffFieldWave` banks the carry on the spot, "so the walk up the lane is what secures it and the trip home is not a second toll"), and the trickle exists so the outbound leg cannot be emptied in advance by standing at the Hatch and killing everything. Every extension below is an elaboration of that one shape, not a replacement for it.

**Built, and unreachable in normal play.** The M102 graybox is built and inert until a dev-only toggle sets `RunSim.FaucetActive`. Its record reports 13 `FaucetLoopTests` green, run 2026-08-24 and re-run green after two refactors; that file sits at `Assets/Tests/EditMode/Run/FaucetLoopTests.cs`, outside the code this entry audits, so the figure is the milestone's word and carries no `## Where` row. The toggle is bound to `Home`, labelled *toggle faucet loop (graybox)*, dispatched by the Dev console into `GameController.ToggleFaucetLoop`; the whole binding table compiles only under `#if UNITY_EDITOR || DEVELOPMENT_BUILD`, so in a player build the binding does not exist, and `_faucetActive` starts false. These are what the shipped loop is still missing from the designed one:

- **Waves auto-chain.** A cycle's Waves keep coming with no further input, one `WaveDirector.InterWaveGap` apart, indefinitely, until the Boarder turns the faucet off — rather than running the zone's authored span and stopping (`FaucetOwnsClear` arming `_faucetChainTimer`, `FaucetChainTick`).
- **Waves hunt the Hatch, not the Boarder.** Proximity still peels a Default transiently and being shot still provokes permanently, but the sight-provoke branch is suppressed for the whole cycle — a sightline pull would stickily drag the stream off the Hatch, which is the grammar the mode retires. The Hatch also takes those hits unscaled, because the field-Wave damage scale is skipped while a cycle is live.
- **Income rides Waves endured, not kills.** Every faucet Wave cleared pays `FaucetLoop.WaveEndureBonus` into the carried pile, split across connected slots. In this slice the shift is partial and stacks: kills still pay on landing at full value ([A Boarding](A-Boarding.md) owns that), and *whether kills pay at all under Waves-endured income* is Open in the brainstorm.
- **A commitment floor.** After `FaucetLoop.MarkAfterWaves` Waves endured the origin terminal is marked and only then does `T` there become legal — **there is no off verb before it**, refused with the count named in the log. Pressed mid-Assault it arms and the draft opens on the clear; pressed in a Prep gap it cancels the pending chain and opens the draft now.
- **A modifier draft that grows with the hold.** One option at the mark, one more per Wave endured past it, capped at `FaucetLoop.DraftCap` (`DraftSize`) — leave at the mark with one option, or endure two more Waves for all three. The options are three axes in reveal order, each one multiplier on the Wave the following bank window will send: **Density** on the Default count (and `enemyCount` with it, keeping the spec invariant), **Toughness** on the health multiplier, **Tempo** on the group gap downward, floored at `WaveDirector.MinSpawnInterval`. That window also gets `FaucetLoop.ConvertBonus` added to its convert multiplier, so one choice raises danger and reward together.
- **The teleport home on confirm.** The Boarder is placed at the Hatch on the draft confirm and a bank window starts immediately, with no `E`-select and with the demo zone's bank gate bypassed on a logged line. The outbound leg was the priced trip; the return is free by design.
- **The co-op vote** — any seat initiates, every seat votes on a visible panel, the party lead confirms, and the teleport is party-wide. Designed, not built: that shape is the brainstorm's (`../Brainstorms/coop-bastion-complexity.md`, *The faucet loop*) and M102's do-not-build list excludes the vote panel. There is no code behind it, and guests are untested with the toggle on.

**Two defects block testing the gated extensions.** Neither touches the shipped loop — with the toggle off no path reads any faucet field, and both defects require it on.

1. **The cycle cannot begin on a default save.** Only `RunSim.TriggerDraw`'s single-draw arm calls `BeginFaucetCycleIfEligible`; `RunSim.Composite.cs`'s `TriggerCompositeDraw` never does. `RealmState.DemoActive` defaults true, and both authored zones run more than one Wave per trigger (`DemoZones.WavesPerTriggerByZone` is `{ 2, 3 }`), so `StartTerminalWave` routes both of them to `StartZoneComposite`. With the toggle on and the shipped demo content in front of the Boarder, no draw reaches the eligibility check at all.
2. **One `T` runs both shutoffs.** `GameController.TickFightInput` calls `TickFaucetAssaultCommands()` and then `TickAssaultShutoffCommand()` in the same frame; `Controls.Down` is a latched per-frame read that neither consumes, and `TickAssaultShutoffCommand` has no faucet guard. So on a save where a cycle *can* begin (a non-demo save, which takes the single-draw arm), one `T` at a marked origin terminal both arms the faucet shutoff and runs the shipped one: the terminal is completed outright, the door opens, the carried pile banks, and `ShutOffFieldWave` → `NotifyAssaultCleared` → `FaucetOwnsClear` then counts the Wave as endured, pays the endure bonus into the pile that was just emptied, sees the arm and opens the draft on the spot. End state: the draft open at an already-complete terminal behind an already-open door, one press after the mark — so the hold-past-the-mark decision the graybox exists to test is never reached.

M102's own playthrough path is stale and is not the way in: it reads *`E`+`T` at T1*, which THE LOOP made impossible the day after M102 landed (a terminal cannot start a Wave, and `T1` is not in the zone table).

Driven by `T` at the Hatch and `T` at the zone's terminal, plus — behind the toggle — `T` at the marked origin and `E`/`T` in the open draft. Shown by the Hatch's start-line prompt (which outranks Convert, Withdraw and Dock in `HatchVerbs.Choose`), the terminal's shutoff row, and, when the graybox is on, the origin terminal's own prompt rows, a worldspace ping on the marked terminal and a HUD status line. Reads back as the door opening and the carried pile banking at the switch, the shutoff and door-open log lines, and — gated — the carried pile rising once per Wave endured, the draft line naming the highlighted axis, and a logged reason for every refusal.

## Consequences

**The objective is positional, so the walk is the content.** Because the terminal ends the Wave, a Boarder cannot finish a zone from the Hatch however well they shoot; and because the trickle holds the lane open once the roster is spent, they cannot empty the lane first and then stroll it. That is a decision the player must account for every zone: hold at the Hatch and let the pile grow, or leave and take the walk while the pile is still unbanked and still bleeding on every Hatch hit ([The Hatch](The-Hatch.md) owns that HP and what it bleeds). The trickle's bodies pay nothing and mend nothing, so stalling in the lane costs time and ammunition and earns neither.

**The shutoff is the bank point at the far end.** Reaching the switch banks the whole carry, which is what makes the outbound trip the priced leg and the trip home free — and it is why a Boarder who dies 60 m up the spine loses a pile they could have secured by pressing on. Removing that would leave the walk risk with no reward attached to its far end.

**It changes what the Bastion's geometry is for.** Spawn points become authored approaches on a lane whose two ends are both verbs, rather than a spawn-distance tuning problem. The gated extensions push that further — the stream walking to the Hatch, and the teleport deliberately removing the return leg's risk rather than the outbound one's.

**Removal test.** Take the loop's shape out and a field Wave has no ending but the last body, the terminal goes back to being a trigger, the walk up the lane stops being the fight the trickle was built to hold open, the carried pile has no bank point at the far end, and nothing opens the zone's door. That is a different game — and it is the game this build stopped being on 2026-08-26.

**Play evidence.** The shipped half has been played: it *is* the loop, and two of its defects were found in play and fixed (the shutoff unreachable during a live wave, and a Dock offer that swallowed the start prompt — both recorded in `BastionVerbs` and `HatchVerbs`). The gated half has none.

M102 is closed on the harness with the playthrough — which *is* the experiment — owed, and the named question is whether the hold decision is real: does a player ever leave at the mark, ever ride to the cap, and when do they choose to travel. That question is **unanswered**, and the two defects above are why it cannot be asked yet.

Whether holding past the mark feels like a bet or a chore, whether the Hatch attrition curve and the draft curve are steep enough to make either end of the hold ever correct, whether standing at the Hatch and mowing converging streams simply beats everything, and whether a Boarder can count the three constants the decision needs (Hatch loss per Wave, unbanked per Wave, trip cost) — all *unknown, needs play data*. The pre-registered kill criteria and what each would falsify are in the brainstorm (`../Brainstorms/coop-bastion-complexity.md`, *What would kill it*).

**Still open, and not resolved here.** Whether the shipped Ante dial survives beside the draft or folds into it (both are live in the slice and they stack), whether the draft grows *options* or *picks*, and whether kills pay at all under endure income. The language table's **field terminal** row is owed a recut when the extensions ship for real, not for the graybox.

## Classification

Core, loop. Serves Expression (`../Design/1-Experience.md` §1.2, 2026-08-16 rank stamp), with Challenge as the vehicle. Expression is the Boarder authoring the stake by choosing when to leave the Hatch and when to end the Wave; the mechanic's Fellowship half is the co-op vote, which is designed, not built (`../Brainstorms/coop-bastion-complexity.md`, *The faucet loop*).

Core by the contract's removal test: taking the shape out returns the terminal to being a trigger, leaves a field Wave with no ending but the last body, and leaves the carried pile with nowhere to bank at the far end of the lane — a different game, reachable by every player today.

Loop by construction (the brainstorm's own reading): the authored spawn rooms and seat set are the fixed cost; the per-cycle decision of when to leave and when to end the wave is the variety.
